# Roadmap

## What this is
BUS102 is a study task list for people learning a language. You add study tasks tagged with the skill they practice, and the site shows which skill you have been avoiding.

## What Done means
A stranger opens the live site, creates an account with an email and password, and adds a few study tasks, each tagged Reading, Writing, Listening, or Speaking. They check tasks off as they finish them. When they sign out and come back later, their tasks are still there. A summary shows how many completed tasks they have per skill and labels the lowest one "Avoiding." Nothing beyond that is part of this project.

## Slices
1. Sign up and log in | done-criteria: (a) On the live site, click Sign up, enter a new fake email and a password, submit; the page shows "Signed in as" and that email. (b) Click Sign out; the email disappears and a Log in button shows. (c) Log in with the same email and password; the email shows again. (d) Log in with that email and a wrong password; an error message appears and you stay logged out. | status: ACTIVE
2. Tasks with skill tags that persist | done-criteria: (a) Logged in, type a task, pick Listening from the skill menu, click Add; the task appears in the list with a Listening label. (b) Check a task's box, reload the page; it still shows as done. (c) Sign out and log back in; every task added before is still there. (d) Log in as a second account; none of the first account's tasks appear. | status: pending
3. Skill summary | done-criteria: (a) On a fresh account, complete one Reading task and two Speaking tasks; the summary shows Reading 1, Speaking 2, Writing 0, Listening 0. (b) On that account, Writing and Listening are both labeled "Avoiding." (c) Uncheck one Speaking task; Speaking drops to 1 without reloading. (d) Log in as a second account; its summary shows only its own counts. | status: pending
